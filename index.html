02 — MANIFESTO

Where sculpture meets street culture. Where the collectible object becomes an emotional monument.

FORM

Repetition as a language. One silhouette, restated until it stops being a toy and starts being a monument.

MATERIAL

Cast vinyl, matte and gloss, seams left visible. Industrial process treated as sculptural craft.

INTENT

Not a store. An archive — where each object is documented, measured and given room to be looked at.

04 — ANATOMY

INSIDE THE ICON.

One half stays sealed. The other is opened like a specimen. The figure is read twice — as a character, and as a manufactured object with a section drawing.

  1. 01

    EXTERNAL SHELL

    Matte cast vinyl, 2.4 mm wall. The surface is deliberately quiet so the silhouette does the talking.

  2. 02

    INNER STRUCTURE

    A full anatomical cast — ribcage, viscera, musculature — moulded at collector scale and painted by hand.

  3. 03

    SIGNATURE ✕✕ EYES

    The mark that made the figure legible worldwide. Two crossed strokes doing the work of an entire face.

  4. 04

    SCULPTURAL MASS

    Weight sits low, in the boots. The stance reads as heavy, still and slightly resigned.
